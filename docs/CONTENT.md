## Projects

### RE17 Control
**Store Operations & Team Workspace**

A digital workspace designed to help store teams monitor and manage daily operations from one place.

Problem context: store information and instructions were scattered across different channels, making it harder to monitor targets, identify priorities, and organize daily work.

Solution direction: a centralized operational workspace for monitoring, daily tasks, schedules, sales/target visibility, stock-opname support, learning, and other store activities.

Public showcase: show the project but never expose confidential store data.
